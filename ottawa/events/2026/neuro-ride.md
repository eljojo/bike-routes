Join us for the inaugural Neuro Ride, a safe, inclusive, and purpose-driven road cycling event in Eastern Ontario, that brings together riders of all levels.

More than just a ride, Neuro Ride brings people together to create meaningful impact. A portion of event proceeds will support services and resources for individuals and families living with mild traumatic brain injury (mTBI) and post-concussion syndrome (PCS).

Choose to ride, walk, or wheel one of three inclusive and accessible paved routes: 9, 28, or 63 km.
After your ride, stay to celebrate with:
·Neuro Ride gift bag pick-up
·Grand prize and donation announcements
·Pay-as-you-go BBQ

Follow us on Instagram for event updates.

Together, let’s create momentum for more accessible brain healthcare, one kilometre at a time.
